The hobby deserves respect — so does the escapement. Roughly **90%** of "I watched a video" damage we see starts with the wrong screwdriver and ends with a scratched screw head and a stripped thread.

## What Is Safe for an Owner to Do Regularly?

Wipe down after sweaty days, keep leather away from drenching, and learn your crown routine — screw-down crowns should be threaded carefully, not forced. If you swim, verify water resistance professionally after any impact or service; guessing depth ratings is expensive.

## What Should You Never Treat as a Weekend Project?

Dial handling, hand removal, jewel pressing, and hairspring manipulation are precision tasks with irreversible failure modes. Even "just opening the case" can compromise dust sealing and introduce finger oils where they should never be.

## How Do You Learn Without Ruining a Watch?

Practice on disposable movements under mentorship — not on a family heirloom. If you love the craft, pursue coursework and proper tooling; if you love the watch, pair curiosity with [repair vs. sell judgment](/guide/is-my-watch-worth-repairing/) before experiments go wrong.
