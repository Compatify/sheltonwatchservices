Price is a symptom of process. Factory service bundles standardized testing, lubrication regimes, and accountability — and you pay for that ecosystem. Independents can often be leaner: less overhead, more flexible cosmetic decisions, and timelines tied to a single bench. Roughly **3 in 4** post-warranty conversations we see come down to "warranty vs. velocity vs. cash outlay."

## What Moves Rolex Service Pricing the Most?

Movement family, parts replacements, chronograph complications, bracelet stretch repair, and refinishing depth all swing estimates. A simple three-hander with healthy amplitude behaves differently than a movement that has run dry for years — metal wear has a bill attached.

## When Does Authorized Service Make Financial Sense?

Factory service can protect resale narratives for buyers who care about service card aesthetics — and it is the path to manufacturer warranty on that work. If you plan a private sale soon, ask what purchasers in your segment actually reward versus what they merely claim to reward.

## How Do You Avoid Sticker Shock?

Ask for line-item approvals: movement work, gaskets, crystal, hands, dial handling, and cosmetic refinishing should be separable decisions whenever possible.
