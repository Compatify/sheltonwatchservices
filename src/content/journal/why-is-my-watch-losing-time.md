Slow running is often progressive. In a sampling mindset, roughly **15–30 seconds per day** might feel tolerable — until you miss a train connection. Track drift over a week, note positions, and bring that log to evaluation; it speeds diagnosis.

## How Does Magnetism Show Up in Real Life?

Speakers, laptops, bag clasps, and tool drawers can magnetize a watch in ordinary routines. The classic clue is erratic rates — fast sometimes, slow sometimes — or sudden changes after travel. A coil-style demagnetizer is not a toy; if you are not experienced, let a watchmaker confirm.

## When Does Losing Time Mean an Overhaul?

When amplitude drops and rate stability disappears across positions, oil is often done doing its job. Regulation alone becomes lipstick on wear — the movement needs cleaning, inspection, and fresh lubrication where pivots demand it.
